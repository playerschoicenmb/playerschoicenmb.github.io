PLAYER'S CHOICE LIVE GOOGLE SHEETS INVENTORY SETUP

1. Upload inventory-template.xlsx to Google Drive and open it as a Google Sheet.
2. Rename the public worksheet tab to Public Inventory.
3. In Google Sheets choose File > Share > Publish to web.
4. In the first dropdown choose Public Inventory, not Entire document.
5. In the format dropdown choose Comma-separated values (.csv).
6. Click Publish and copy the generated URL.
7. Open inventory-config.js.
8. Replace PASTE_PUBLISHED_GOOGLE_SHEETS_CSV_URL_HERE with that complete URL. Keep the quotation marks.
9. Upload inventory-config.js and inventory.js to the repository.
10. In inventory.html, make sure inventory-config.js loads before inventory.js as shown in inventory-html-patch.txt.

AFTER THIS ONE-TIME SETUP
Employees only edit the Google Sheet. The website reloads the sheet whenever the Inventory page opens, whenever a visitor returns to the browser tab, and every five minutes while the page remains open.

PRIVACY
Only publish the Public Inventory tab. Do not put supplier cost, profit margin, customer data, security details, or internal notes in the published tab.

FALLBACK
Until a real Google URL is pasted into inventory-config.js, the demo automatically continues using inventory.csv.
